How the totals work

Money received counts payments made during the selected month.

Dues covered counts amounts assigned to the selected month, even if the money was received earlier.

Outstanding shows the selected month’s unpaid dues. Cumulative arrears includes all unpaid dues from joining through the selected month, capped at the last billable month.
